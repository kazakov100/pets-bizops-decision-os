Lemonade's "Synthetic Agents" program (launched July 2023 with General
Catalyst) is a customer-acquisition-cost (CAC) financing arrangement: in
Lemonade's own words, "Synthetic Agents (GC) will finance up to 80% of our
CAC spend in any given month... and will receive a 'commission' of up to
16% from, and only from, the premiums paid by the corresponding cohort"
(the customers who joined that month). Lemonade frames it as "time-shifters,
funneling customers' future gross profits backwards in time, so customers
effectively finance their own CAC before we've even met them" -- breaking
the usual "growth-depletes-cash" tension and enabling capital-light growth
without ceding gross profit for the customer's lifetime the way traditional
agents would. Lemonade pairs two symbiotic capital partners: reinsurers
relieve its regulatory-capital burden through the quota-share program, and
General Catalyst relieves its working-capital burden through Synthetic
Agents. Strategically this is a financing/growth-funding lever, NOT an
underwriting or monetization one -- it changes how aggressively new-customer
growth can be funded without straining cash, but doesn't touch loss ratio or
premium-per-customer. So for a growth-vs-monetization gap (new customers
outgrowing premium-per-customer), the right lever is pricing or cross-sell,
not CAC financing.

Source: Lemonade, Inc., "Synthetic Agents" (company blog) -- https://www.lemonade.com/blog/synthetic-agents/
